# BlogCard

`BlogCard` renders one item from the Blog list design. It keeps the supplied Figma image, tag, metadata, and disabled “Learn More” affordance while blog detail routing is out of scope.

The card is 465px wide in the desktop two-column list and 330px wide in the mobile single-column list. Its content contract is `BlogPost` from `src/modules/content/lib/blog-posts.ts`.
